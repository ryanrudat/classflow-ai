Read the evidence record · 閱讀證據紀錄

Bonn-Oberkassel, Germany · about 14,000 years ago. This card focuses on the younger dog in the study.

What researchers found · 研究發現

The remains of a young dog were buried with two adult humans. Researchers examined damage in its teeth.

詞語提示｜remains 遺骸；buried 被埋葬；adult humans 成年人；damage 損傷。

What researchers think · 研究者的推測

The damage suggests serious illness. The dog seems to have survived for weeks while ill. The researchers think survival without human help would have been unlikely.

詞語提示｜serious illness 嚴重疾病；survived 活了下來；while ill 生病期間；without 沒有；unlikely 不太可能。

The burial information, age and illness interpretation cannot be read directly from this photograph alone.

資料來源｜分清楚「照片中看得見的細節」和「研究紀錄提供的資訊」，不要把兩者混在一起。

Compare with Kae: 15,000 years ago; a wolf; sleeping by the fire; accepting children. Which part can this evidence help us check? What remains uncertain?

比較提示｜比較故事與證據紀錄中的時間、動物和細節。哪一部分可以查證？哪一部分仍不確定？

The cave location · 洞穴位置

Lascaux, France. This map relates to photograph 1. It does not show Chauvet.

Simplified map. Not to scale. The gallery is marked, not the exact position of the horse.

讀圖提示｜entrance 入口；gallery 洞穴中的廊道／區域；not to scale 未按實際比例繪製。用手指從入口沿路線走到壁畫所在區域；地圖只對應 Lascaux，不是 Chauvet。

Read the Lascaux research note · 閱讀 Lascaux 的研究說明

Tests on animal bone from Lascaux place a period of human use at about 21,000–21,500 years ago. This is not a direct test of the photographed paint.

年代提示｜測量的是動物骨頭，用來估計人類曾使用洞穴的時期；不是直接測照片中的顏料。

Lascaux is an earlier example than Kae’s story. These records do not identify the artist or establish what happened at Kae’s particular camp. The Chauvet photograph is a separate example from a different cave; the Lascaux map and dating note do not apply to it.

Evidence card C

Could the dates overlap? · 時間可能重疊嗎？

Here, “dinosaurs” means the extinct non-bird dinosaurs, such as T. rex. Birds are living dinosaurs and are not included in this claim check.

詞語提示｜non-bird dinosaurs 非鳥類恐龍；died out 滅絕；years ago ＿＿年前。

讀圖提示｜66,000,000 = 6,600 萬；15,000 = 1 萬 5 千；30,000 = 3 萬。上圖以「百萬年」為單位。下圖只放大最近 3 萬年，不是接在上圖後面的另一段時間。

Find Kae. Did the non-bird dinosaurs die out before or after his time? Could he hunt a living one? Use both dates.

比較提示｜找出 Kae 的年代，再比較恐龍滅絕的年代。before 在……之前；after 在……之後。請用兩個年代說明理由，寫在第 6 頁。

The event positions are proportional within each line. The lower line is a zoom, not an equal-length second time period. Marker sizes are enlarged for visibility. Extinction date: Natural History Museum. Kae’s date: the fictional Recording 001.

Read the museum note · 閱讀博物館說明

Olduvai Gorge, Tanzania · about 1.8 million years old.

Small pieces were struck off this stone, leaving an edge.

詞語提示｜struck off 敲掉；leaving an edge 留下一個邊緣。請把資料中的描述與照片中的特徵連起來。

I can see… / This may show… because… / I cannot tell… from this evidence.

句型提示｜我看見＿＿。／這可能表示＿＿，因為＿＿。／只靠這些證據，我還無法判斷＿＿。請在第 7 頁完成一份報告。

Read the research note · 閱讀研究說明

Qesem Cave, Israel · about 300,000 years ago.

Laboratory tests identified several layers of wood ash and heated bone. Stone tools were found nearby.

詞語提示｜laboratory tests 實驗室檢測；layers 層；wood ash 木灰；heated bone 受熱的骨頭；nearby 附近。

Which part of the information is a visible observation? Which part comes from expert analysis?

資料來源｜哪些是眼睛看得到的觀察？哪些是專家分析後才知道的資訊？

What might the combination of findings suggest? What does it not tell us? Complete one Silent Evidence Report, not both.

思考提示｜這些發現合在一起，可能表示什麼？還不能判斷什麼？A 或 B 只做一份；老師指定 B 時才改做 B。
